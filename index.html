FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is JBooks?

JBooks is a cloud-based accounting and business-management solution for bookkeeping, invoicing, expenses, inventory and financial reporting.

Who is JBooks for?

JBooks is positioned for Sri Lankan startups, small businesses, growing teams and larger organizations that need a connected accounting workflow.

Can I access JBooks online?

Yes. JBooks is presented as a cloud-based platform, allowing business financial information to be accessed through the online system.

Does JBooks support invoicing and inventory?

Yes. JBooks combines professional invoicing and billing with product, stock, purchase and sales tracking.

Does JBooks support VAT reporting and multiple users?

Yes. VAT reporting is included in the current plan structure, with automated VAT reporting in Professional. User limits vary by plan, with unlimited users in Enterprise.

How do I get started?

Create an account, import your business data and start managing invoices, expenses, inventory and financial reporting from the dashboard.
